Furnished Premises

The Premises are rented furnished. A written inventory of all furnished items and their condition shall be completed and signed by both parties during the move-in walkthrough and attached as Exhibit A — Furnished Inventory. Tenant shall be responsible for any damage beyond normal wear and tear.

Utilities

Tenant is responsible for establishing and paying all utilities in Tenant's name, including electricity, gas, water/sewer, trash, and internet. Landlord is not responsible for utility service interruptions.

Lawn, Snow Removal & Exterior Maintenance

Tenant is responsible for all lawn care and snow removal throughout the Lease Term, including mowing, edging, leaf removal, and snow/ice removal from walkways and driveways. Failure to maintain the exterior may result in Landlord arranging service at Tenant's expense with written notice.

Occupants

Only Thomas Potter and Haley Christmann are authorized to reside at the Premises. No additional occupants are permitted without prior written consent of the Landlord.

Guests

Guests may stay at the Premises for no more than 14 consecutive days or 30 days total in any 12-month period without prior written approval from the Landlord. Guests exceeding these limits shall be considered unauthorized occupants.

Pets

No pets are permitted on the Premises without prior written consent of the Landlord. Unauthorized pets are grounds for lease termination.

Illegal Activity & Drug Policy

Tenant shall not engage in, permit, or facilitate any illegal activity on or near the Premises, including but not limited to the use, manufacture, distribution, or storage of illegal drugs or controlled substances. Violation of this provision constitutes a material breach of this Agreement and grounds for immediate termination of tenancy in accordance with South Dakota law. Tenant shall be liable for any fines, damages, or legal fees incurred by Landlord as a result of Tenant's illegal activity.

Smoking Policy

Smoking of any substance (tobacco, marijuana, or electronic cigarettes/vaping) is prohibited inside the Premises. Smoking is permitted outside only, at a reasonable distance from the structure and windows. Tenant shall be responsible for any damage or odor caused by indoor smoking.

Subletting & Assignment

Tenant may not sublet the Premises, assign this Agreement, or permit any other person to occupy the Premises without prior written consent of the Landlord. Unauthorized subletting is grounds for immediate termination.

Renter's Insurance

Tenant shall obtain and maintain a renter's insurance policy throughout the Lease Term with a minimum of $100,000 in personal liability coverage. Proof of insurance shall be provided to Landlord prior to move-in and upon renewal. Landlord's insurance does not cover Tenant's personal property or liability.

Early Termination

Should Tenant terminate this Agreement prior to the end of the Lease Term, Tenant shall provide a minimum of 30 days written notice and pay an early termination fee of $3,900.00 (two months' rent). This fee does not release Tenant from liability for rent owed through the end of the notice period.

Landlord Entry

Landlord shall provide a minimum of 24 hours' advance notice before entering the Premises, except in cases of emergency.

Maintenance & Repairs

Tenant shall maintain the Premises in a clean, sanitary condition and promptly notify Landlord of any needed repairs. Tenant is responsible for minor repairs under $100 caused by Tenant's negligence. Landlord is responsible for maintaining the Premises in habitable condition per South Dakota law.

Alterations

Tenant shall make no alterations, additions, or improvements without prior written consent of the Landlord. All approved alterations become the property of the Landlord upon termination unless otherwise agreed in writing.

Parking

Tenant is permitted to park one (1) vehicle on the Premises. Tenant shall not park on the lawn or in unauthorized areas.

Joint and Several Liability

All Tenants signing this Agreement are jointly and severally liable for all obligations, including rent, damages, and fees.

Holdover

If Tenant remains after lease expiration without written consent of Landlord, such holdover constitutes a month-to-month tenancy at the same rent and terms. Either party may terminate with 30 days written notice.

Notices

All notices shall be in writing delivered by personal delivery, first-class mail, or email with confirmation of receipt.
